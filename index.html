Selected research

  • ACM UIST 2026 CORE 2023: A*

    CLOAK — privacy across social-media posts

    Individual posts can appear harmless while revealing sensitive details when read together. CLOAK uses a multi-agent workflow to analyze posting history and a new draft, explain these combined risks, and propose edits the author can review before sharing.

  • IEEE CBMI 2025

    ReViewQwen — explainable multimodal comparison

    A fine-tuned vision-language model that compares product descriptions and images with buyer reviews and photographs. It distinguishes actual discrepancies from agreement and expectations outside the seller's claims, and generates an explanation for its assessment.

  • IEEE Aerospace Conference 2026

    AeroAssist — reasoning across inspection evidence

    Introduces Guided Reflective Diagnostic Reasoning, a structured prompting method for examining aircraft inspection video, audio, and sensor logs together. The method guides the model through evidence comparison and reflection before producing its assessment.
